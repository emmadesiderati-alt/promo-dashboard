🔑 EU Key Insights

Weighted Blanket: AT’s untapped EU export

AT’s Free Weighted Blanket achieves +28.7% with 7 reliable runs. CH has 2 directional runs at +33.3% and the highest GM% of any CH mechanic (70.6%). Yet DE, ES, FR, and PT have never tested it. AT should be the EU reference market for a coordinated rollout test in Q3–Q4.

Cart Discount is the EU’s most under-explored mechanic

Proven in DE (+10.9%), IT (+42.6%), and FR (top performer at +43–57%). Yet BE, CH, and PT have zero runs. AT has 2 inconclusive runs. Given FR’s consistent outperformance, this is the single highest-priority transfer for markets without it.

5% vs 10% discount depth: market-specific, not global

AT and BE respond to 5% Extra Discount. CH and PT use 10% as standard — 5% types in CH go deeply negative (−11% to −43%). Any market considering a depth change should reference its own data. Depth preference is market-calibrated.

Free Pillow: universal mechanic with an IT exception

Free Pillow-Mattress is strong across BE (+194%), PT (+82%), CH (+23.5%), AT (+10.2%). But IT has run it 43 times at essentially baseline — a clear example of diminishing returns from over-rotation. Frequency management is as critical as mechanic selection.

FR is structurally different — bundle composition is the lever

FR is the only market where accessory richness in the bundle composition drives performance. Full Bedroom Set + Linen outperforms simpler bed bundles by +70 percentage points (NR lift +74% vs +4%). Discount depth is secondary in FR — the content of the bundle is what sells.

Peak day patterns: Sunday standard · ES/PT → Monday exception

AT, BE, CH, IT peak on Sunday — Sun → Mon or Sun → Tue are the universal best windows. ES and PT both peak on Monday, aligned with their DoW rotation ending day. FR regular season also peaks Monday. Urgency close timing cannot be standardised across the EU.

DE: scope matters more than mechanic

In DE, the key decision is All Mattresses vs One Mattress scope. All Mattresses drives NR (+8.7%); One Mattress protects GM% (54.2% vs 51.1%). The business objective must be defined before choosing scope, every single cycle.

Flash Sale: CH-exclusive, worth a transfer test

Flash Sale is the only mechanic in exactly one EU market (CH, 8 runs, +19.1%, 65% GM%). It is not structurally blocked from other markets. Given IT’s strong Cart Discount response, a Flash Sale pilot in IT could be a meaningful 2026 test.
